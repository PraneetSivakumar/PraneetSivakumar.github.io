Case 01 · Retail project · Complete

The Case of the Cancelled Orders

1 in 13 online orders never reached the customer. Why?

Data
15,000+ transactions, £72k revenue, 8 stores (sample retail dataset)
Approach
Cleaned and explored in Python (Pandas); cancellations by category and month
Answer
Health & Beauty cancelled most, at 10%. Loyalty members spend 8% more; December runs 21% above average
Output
5-view Tableau dashboard with stock and promotion recommendations

Case 02 · Personal finance · Simulated data

The Real Price

What did my spending cost in hours of my life?

Data
707 transactions, Jan – Oct 2026, simulated from my real routine (internship, retail shifts)
Approach
Excel, SQL and Python: what, when, why and how I spend; every £ converted into hours of work
Answer
1.6× more spending in the 3 days after payday (p < 0.001); every shift-day takeaway came after 9pm, costing 71 hours of work
Output
Interactive dashboard, a Spending Wrapped story and a what-if plan worth 131 hours a year
